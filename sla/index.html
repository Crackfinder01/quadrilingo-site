Support policy (SLA)

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Quadrilingo is built and supported by one independent developer in Switzerland. This page states what we commit to, and what we do not. It applies to all plans, including the free tier and trials.

Channel, languages and hours

  • Channel: e-mail to support@quadrilingo.com. There is no phone support.
  • Languages: English, French and German.
  • Hours: Monday–Friday, 9:00–17:00 Swiss time, excluding Swiss public holidays. Messages received outside these hours are handled on the next business day.
  • First reply: within 1 business day, for every request.

Severity levels

We use these levels to decide what to work on first. The first-reply target is the same for every level; higher levels are handled before lower ones.

LevelExamplesWhat we do
1 – CriticalNobody on your site can translate or update pages; a suspected security issue or exposure of dataFirst reply within 1 business day. Handled before anything else until it is fixed or a workaround is in place. We keep you informed of progress.
2 – HighA main feature fails for some spaces or users and there is no reasonable workaroundFirst reply within 1 business day. Handled right after level 1 issues.
3 – NormalA problem with a workaround; a question about how the app works or about your planFirst reply within 1 business day.
4 – LowSuggestions, wording, cosmetic issuesFirst reply within 1 business day. Considered for future versions; no delivery date promised.

We assign the level based on the impact you describe. Tell us if you think a request deserves a higher level.

Security

  • Report vulnerabilities to security@quadrilingo.com. We acknowledge them within 3 business days and fix them within the timeframes of the Atlassian Marketplace Security Bug Fix Policy (critical 10 days, high 4 weeks, medium 12 weeks, low 25 weeks).
  • If we discover a security incident affecting the app or customer data, we notify Atlassian within 24 hours of discovering it, as Atlassian requires, and we notify affected customers without undue delay, and where possible within 48 hours of becoming aware of an incident affecting their personal data. Details: incident response and our Data Processing Agreement.

What we do not offer

  • No 24/7 support and no phone support.
  • No uptime commitment: the app depends on Atlassian's Forge platform, Confluence Cloud and DeepL (see our terms).
  • No guaranteed resolution time, except for security vulnerabilities as stated above.
  • No access to your site: we never ask for your password, an API token or admin access.

Billing and refunds

Subscriptions, cancellations and refunds are handled by Atlassian through the Marketplace, under Atlassian's policies.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
