Space settings

Translation is turned on space by space. Each space has its own original language, translation languages and options. Labels in bold quotes are the ones on the screen (English version; the screen follows your Confluence language).

Where to find them

In the space settings, open Quadrilingo.

Only space admins see and change these settings. Other people don't get a read-only view: if they open the screen, they see “Only administrators of this space can change these settings.”

Good to know

  • Changing the languages under “Translate into” does not delete existing translations. Removing a language stops new translations and automatic updates into it.
  • Turning translation off does not delete any page. Existing translations stay as normal Confluence pages. While it is off, edits of original pages are not tracked: after you turn it on again, each page is checked the next time its original is edited, and until then its translations can still show Up to date.
  • Turning on “Update translations automatically” does not update pages that are already outdated right away: each one is updated the next time its original is edited. Update them from the dialog to catch up now.
  • Pages written in another language than the space's original language: a space has one original language. If a page clearly looks like it is written in another language, the “Translate this page” dialog warns you before you translate (short or mixed-language pages get no warning).
  • Automatic updates use your monthly allowance. On a busy space, keep them off and update translations when a page is stable. See Quotas and plans.
  • If the original language of existing translations cannot be checked (rare), you can still turn translation off; other changes and turning it on wait until it can be checked again.
  • Changing a Swiss variant (Swiss German or Swiss French) applies to the next translations and updates. Existing translations made in the other variant then show as “Outdated” in the “Translate this page” dialog and the language menu (the note under the page title and the admin dashboard catch up at the next change in that page family): update them from the “Translate this page” dialog, or they follow at the next change of their original page when automatic updates are on. Switching between French and Swiss French usually uses none of your monthly volume; switching between German and Swiss German translates the page again and counts like any update.
  • Passages to review (“À relire” in French): after a translation, the “Translate this page” dialog can show “Show the passages to review” for a language. Each passage gives the reason (a number or currency differs, an element possibly misplaced, or “Swiss usage not applied here (meal or CHF amount): to review”), then the original and the translated text. These are hints, not errors: check the passage in the translation and correct it by hand if needed.
  • Two people editing at once: if someone else saved the settings after you opened the screen, saving is refused and you are asked to reload them.

Site-wide overview (Confluence admins)

The admin dashboard, Confluence settings → Quadrilingo, shows how much of the monthly allowance is used and lists the spaces where Quadrilingo is set up (on or off) that you can see: their languages, whether automatic updates are on, and how many translations are Up to date, Outdated, Edited by hand, Error, Volume used up or In progress. Spaces never set up are not listed. For each space, “Pages to review” lists its pages with an error, edited by hand or outdated (with links to the pages you can open; no page titles), and “Settings and glossary” opens this screen. There is no e-mail alert.
