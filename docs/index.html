Quadrilingo documentation

Quadrilingo keeps Confluence Cloud pages in English, French, German and Italian, linked to each other. Each translation is a real Confluence page under its original, marked Outdated when the original changes, and updated from the page menu or automatically.

Start here

  • Getting started: install the app and translate your first page.
  • Space settings: turn translation on for a space, choose its original language, translation languages, Swiss variants, tone and automatic updates.

Daily use

  • Translate a page: where translations go, what is translated and what is not.
  • Outdated translations: how translations are kept up to date, manually or automatically, and how hand edits are protected.
  • Language switcher: how readers move between language versions.
  • Glossary: names never to translate and required translations, per space.

Reference and help

  • Quotas and plans: prices, monthly translation allowance and what counts.
  • Troubleshooting: messages you may see and what to do.
  • FAQ: common questions about languages, quality, data and billing.

Need help?

Write to support@quadrilingo.com in English, French or German. We reply within 1 business day (Monday–Friday, 9:00–17:00 Swiss time). See the support page.
