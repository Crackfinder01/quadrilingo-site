Sub-processors — Quadrilingo

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Thibaut Keller uses the following sub-processors to provide Quadrilingo. Each sub-processor is bound by written data protection terms. Our Data Processing Agreement explains how we notify customers before we add or replace a sub-processor.

Changes

We will publish any new or replacing sub-processor on this page at least 30 days before it starts processing customer data, and inform the technical contacts of our customers by e-mail. A customer may object as described in our Data Processing Agreement.
