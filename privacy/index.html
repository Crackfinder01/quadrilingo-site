Privacy Policy — Quadrilingo

Last updated: 17 November 2026

1. Who we are

Quadrilingo (the “App”) is provided by Thibaut Keller, Route des Riaux 1, 1782 Lossy, Switzerland (“we”, “us”, “our”).

  • Privacy questions and requests: privacy@quadrilingo.com
  • Security issues: security@quadrilingo.com

We, and not Atlassian, are responsible for the processing of personal data described in this policy. Atlassian's own privacy policy governs Atlassian's products, including Confluence Cloud and the Atlassian Marketplace.

2. Scope

This policy explains:

  1. how the App handles data from your Confluence Cloud site when we process it on behalf of our customers (sections 3 to 6); and
  2. how we handle personal data for our own purposes, for example licensing, support and security communications (section 7).

It applies to Switzerland (Federal Act on Data Protection, “FADP”), the European Union and the European Economic Area (General Data Protection Regulation, “GDPR”), and any other country where the App is used, to the extent the local law applies.

3. Our role

  • Content processed by the App (page text, page titles, glossary entries, settings): your organisation (the customer that installed the App) is the controller. We act as a processor and only process this data to provide the App, following your organisation's instructions and our Data Processing Agreement. If you are a user of a Confluence site, your organisation's privacy notice applies to you; please contact your organisation first.
  • Our own business data (section 7): we are the controller.

4.2 What the App does not do

  • It does not read comments, attachments, user profiles or e-mail addresses of your users. (It only receives from Confluence the language and time zone of the signed-in user, see 4.1, and does not store them.)
  • It does not put user account IDs in its translation jobs or its storage. The App checks the permissions of the user who starts a translation, then performs the translation under its own app identity.
  • Before text leaves your page for translation, the App removes code blocks, macro parameters, user mentions, links, e-mail addresses, URLs and phone numbers, and replaces them with neutral placeholders. They are put back locally afterwards.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking or advertising tools.
  • It does not sell personal data and does not use your content to train any AI model. We do not run our own servers.

4.3 Translation engine

Translations are produced by DeepL SE (Cologne, Germany) through its paid API, under our own DeepL contract and data processing agreement. You do not need a DeepL account. The App sends DeepL only the text segments to be translated, the page title, short context (for example the heading above a paragraph) and, if the space has a glossary, that glossary: the whole glossary of the space is stored in our DeepL account as a DeepL glossary so that DeepL can apply it (with a second copy of its entries into German, using the Swiss terms, if the space translates into Swiss Standard German). According to DeepL, texts sent through its paid API are not stored permanently, are deleted after translation and are not used to train its models; DeepL may keep them for a short time (according to its terms, at most 72 hours) if needed to handle an error. Glossaries are stored in our DeepL account under a random name, re-created every 25 days while they are in use, deleted as soon as they are replaced, and in any case deleted at the latest 31 days after they were created (see 6). DeepL may process data in the European Union, in the United States and in other regions where it uses Amazon Web Services infrastructure, including the Asia-Pacific region (see 9 and our sub-processor list).

5. Where data is hosted

  • Your Confluence site: translated pages and their links are ordinary Confluence pages and properties. They stay under your administrators' control and follow your site's Atlassian data residency settings.
  • Atlassian Forge: the App runs on Atlassian's Forge platform. Its storage and queues are hosted by Atlassian. Atlassian aims to run the App in the same location as your site, but may sometimes run it elsewhere for reliability, performance or security reasons.
  • Translation engine: see 4.3.

8. Who receives personal data

We do not sell or rent personal data. We share it only with:

  • Atlassian (hosting of the App on Forge; Marketplace licensing and billing);
  • our translation engine provider (see 4.3) for the content to be translated;
  • Infomaniak Network SA (Switzerland) for our e-mail (support and security messages);
  • GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages, USA) for our website;
  • our accountant or fiduciary, for accounting records;
  • authorities, if we are legally required to do so.

The current list of sub-processors for App data is published at quadrilingo.com/subprocessors.

9. International transfers

We are based in Switzerland. The European Commission recognises that Switzerland provides an adequate level of data protection. Personal data may be processed in the following countries:

  • Switzerland (us);
  • the countries where Atlassian hosts your site and the Forge platform according to your site's data residency settings, and the countries named in Atlassian's sub-processor list, which include the United States. Atlassian relies on the EU-U.S. and Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and on Standard Contractual Clauses;
  • Germany and other EU countries (DeepL SE is established in Germany), the United States and other countries in which DeepL uses Amazon Web Services infrastructure, including in the Asia-Pacific region. According to DeepL, its transfers outside Europe rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses;
  • Switzerland for our e-mail provider, Infomaniak Network SA;
  • the United States for our website host, GitHub, Inc. (website server logs only).

The EU/EEA countries are recognised by Switzerland as providing an adequate level of protection, and the United States are recognised for companies certified under the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. When personal data is transferred to another country without an adequate level of protection under Swiss or EU law, the transfer relies on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (with the amendments required for Swiss law), concluded by us or by our sub-processor with its own sub-processors.

10. Security

We protect data with the measures described on our security page: the App runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform, uses only the Confluence permissions it needs, encrypts data in transit, keeps secrets in encrypted Forge variables, and never writes page content to logs. No system is completely secure. If a security incident affects your data, we will inform your organisation without undue delay.

11. Your rights

Depending on the applicable law, you may have the right to access your personal data, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to its processing, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw consent at any time.

  • For data processed by the App on behalf of your organisation (sections 3 to 6), please contact your organisation. We will help it respond.
  • For data we process as controller (section 7), write to privacy@quadrilingo.com. We may ask you to prove your identity.

You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Switzerland, the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC, www.edoeb.admin.ch); in the EU/EEA, the authority of your country of residence or work.
