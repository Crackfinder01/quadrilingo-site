Why teams use Quadrilingo

  • Translated pages that stay in sync

    When the original page changes, its translations are marked Outdated. Update them from the page menu, or automatically. Unchanged passages are reused, and pages edited by hand are never overwritten automatically.

  • Translation included, nothing to set up

    No API key to buy, no translation account to create, no separate contract. Install, choose your languages, translate your first page. Each plan includes a monthly translation allowance.

  • Switch language from the page title

    Under the page title, readers open the language menu, see which versions are up to date and open theirs. Translations are normal Confluence pages: searchable, exportable, and covered by your existing permissions.

What it does

Many teams write documentation in one language and need it in others. Copying pages by hand or pasting machine translations works once, then the versions drift apart. Quadrilingo keeps your Confluence pages in English, French, German and Italian, and keeps them linked.

  • Linked translations. Translate a page from the ••• menu (Translate this page). Each translation is a child page of its source, with a translated title. The app remembers which page is the translation of which, in which language, and from which version of the source.
  • Outdated detection. When the content of the source page changes, its translations are marked Outdated within a few minutes, in the label under the title, the language menu and the Translate this page dialog. Comments don't count. Requires an active subscription or trial.
  • Updates from the page menu or automatic. On the original page, Translate this page ticks the outdated languages for you. Or a space admin turns on Update translations automatically: about 10 minutes after the original changes, existing translations are updated, using the monthly translation volume. Automatic updates never create new pages.
  • Hand edits respected. A translation someone edited directly shows Edited by hand and is never overwritten automatically. It is replaced only if a user explicitly chooses to.
  • Layout preserved. Titles, paragraphs, headings, lists, tables, panels, quotes, tasks, expand titles, captions, status labels and image alt text are translated. Code blocks, inline code, macro settings, link addresses, mentioned people, e-mail addresses and phone numbers are never sent for translation.
  • Glossary per space. Names that must never be translated and required translations per language, with a Swiss Standard German column. Space admins edit it in the space settings, or paste it as CSV.
  • Language menu. Under each translated page's title, readers see every version with its status and an Open link. It runs with the reader's permissions: a version they cannot open shows Not available, without a link.
  • Admin overview. Translation status per space and the month's consumption, in the Confluence administration.
  • Swiss variants and tone. Per space: Swiss Standard German (“ss” instead of “ß”), Swiss French (“dîner” at noon, “souper” in the evening, amounts as “CHF 0.70”), and a formal tone (vous, Sie, Lei) by default.
  • Passages to review. After each translation, the dialog flags passages worth a second look (a number or currency that may differ, a protected name that may have been translated), with a short excerpt of the original and of the translation.

Who it is for

Teams that run their wiki in more than one language: Swiss companies working in French, German, Italian and English; Belgian, Canadian and international teams; HR, IT, quality and support teams whose policies and how-tos must exist in each language.

What it does not do (yet)

  • Languages other than English, French, German and Italian. A space has one German variant (standard or Swiss), not both side by side.
  • Human review workflow, translation of comments or attachments, translation of a whole space in one go.
  • Side-by-side view of source and translation.

Your data

To translate a page, Quadrilingo sends its text to DeepL SE, our translation provider, on a paid DeepL API plan. It does not send code blocks, macro settings, link addresses, mentioned people, e-mail addresses or phone numbers. Translations are saved as pages in your Confluence. Settings, glossary and translation memory are kept in Atlassian-hosted app storage (Forge).

Questions

Do I need a DeepL, Google or OpenAI account?

No. Translation is included in the subscription. You never enter an API key.

Are translations real pages?

Yes. Each translation is a child page of the original, with a translated title. Search, page tree, PDF/Word export and permissions work as usual.

What if I correct a translation by hand?

The app notices it: the translation shows Edited by hand and is never overwritten automatically. To replace it anyway, tick its language and Also replace translations that were edited by hand in the Translate this page dialog.

Does changing the glossary update existing translations?

No, nothing is re-translated on its own. The glossary applies to the next translations and updates.

Do I need Rovo?

No. The app works with or without Rovo.

Does it work on Confluence Data Center or Server?

No. Confluence Cloud only.
