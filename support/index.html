Support

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Contact us

  • E-mail: support@quadrilingo.com
  • Languages: English, French, German (français, Deutsch)
  • Hours: Monday–Friday, 9:00–17:00 Swiss time, excluding Swiss public holidays
  • First reply: within 1 business day

Security and privacy

  • Security reports (vulnerabilities, suspected incidents): security@quadrilingo.com. See our vulnerability disclosure policy.
  • Privacy requests (access, correction, deletion of personal data we hold as controller): privacy@quadrilingo.com. See our privacy policy.

Before you write

Many questions are answered in the documentation, in particular Troubleshooting and the FAQ.

What to include

To help us answer quickly, please include:

  • your site URL (https://yourcompany.atlassian.net);
  • the page ID or link, whether it is the original or a translation, the languages involved, and the approximate time with your time zone;
  • what you expected and what you saw, with the exact message or status shown (a screenshot of the dialog helps; please hide anything confidential).

You don't need to send us the page content. We never ask for your Atlassian password, an API token or admin access to your site.

Billing, cancellation and refunds

Quadrilingo is bought and billed through the Atlassian Marketplace, so subscriptions, cancellations and refunds are handled by Atlassian under its own policies. See Atlassian's refund documentation. Translated pages stay in your Confluence after you cancel or uninstall.
